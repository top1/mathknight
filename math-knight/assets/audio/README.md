# MathKnight Audio Assets

This directory contains the background music (BGM), sound effects (SFX), and fanfares for **MathKnight**.

All assets are managed and dynamically loaded/synthesized by `res://scripts/autoload/AudioManager.gd`.

## 🎵 Background Music & Jingles
- **`bgm_menu.wav`**: Cozy medieval fantasy tavern theme with acoustic lute arpeggios, playful flute melodies, and hand percussion (105 BPM loop).
- **`bgm_battle.wav`**: Driving heroic chiptune / arcade battle music (135 BPM loop) with bassline, synth leads, and punchy drums.
- **`bgm_boss.wav`**: Dramatic, fast boss fight track (150 BPM loop) with heavy war drums, staccato brass stabs, and urgent minor arpeggios.
- **`jingle_victory.wav`**: Celebratory victory fanfare with bright brass trumpets and sparkling crystal chimes.

## 🔊 Sound Effects (SFX)
- **`sfx_bubble_pop.wav`**: Crisp downward sine bubble pop with instant attack for slicing math bubbles.
- **`sfx_sword_slash.wav`**: Sharp sword swoosh with filtered noise sweep and bass transient.
- **`sfx_correct.wav`**: Sparkling rewarding major chord chime with bell harmonics (pitch scales dynamically with combo streak).
- **`sfx_wrong.wav`**: Low dual-detuned sawtooth error buzz.
- **`sfx_coin.wav`**: Bright 2-note arcade gold jingle (B5 -> E6).
- **`sfx_diamond.wav`**: Magical 4-note ascending crystal chime arpeggio with vibrato.
- **`sfx_click.wav`**: Snappy mechanical UI button click.
- **`sfx_levelup.wav`**: Heroic rising 4-note fanfare arpeggio with full major chord sustain.
- **`sfx_chest_open.wav`**: Latch click, wooden creak, and opening shimmer.
- **`sfx_chest_break.wav`**: Low impact thud and wooden splinter crack on chest failure.
